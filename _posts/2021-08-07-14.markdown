There's information and disinformation. There's Left and there's Right. There's Fox News and there's CNN. The choices all around us are clear. And for the most part, we understand the choice we are making and why we are making it. However, this does not appear to be the case with something so clearly objective in which we all participate in at all times: reality.

Many are living in different realities. While differing opinions and preferences don't normally cause conflict, the the clashing of differing realities causes the parties involved to lose a grip on what's actually true. This clashing normally arises in the very place which I believe is the main cause for the multi-reality condondrum: social media.

It's rare to find someone who spends exorbitant of time on social media and is also simply...reasonable. As humans, we become a product of our environments. And when our environments are as fleeting, inconsequentially dismissive, and dehumanizing of others, it becomes clear why we've become what we consume. Toxicity in - toxicity out.

Get off of social media and talk with people in person. Remember your flaws before pointing out theirs. Fight to be kind rather than being quick to be right.
