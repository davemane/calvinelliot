<h2>What countries are bending the curve?</h2>
<h3>A few things are important to consider when looking at the following graph:</h3>
<h4>
  <ul style="list-style-type:disc;">
  <li>confirmed cases measures the state of an outbreak in a country <i>and</i> also how agressively that country is testing</li>
  <li>this is a log scale, so just looking at a trend without proper reference can be misleading</li>
  <li>this chart does not measure the infection rate by taking into account population size, but rather totals</li>
  <li>the x-axis does not measure time, but rather the number of days since confirmed cases first reached 30 per day</li>
</ul>
</h4>
