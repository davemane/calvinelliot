This is a completely subjective ordering of 122 The Apprentice candidates according to yours truly. Regardless of your personal thoughts concerning the show's host, The Apprentice was a pretty great show. The ranking is formulated on a per-candidate basis in my head and taking into consideration the following factors: smarts, skills, likeability, integrity, and any other X factors that pop into my head.
*Note* the ranking is only for non-celebrity seasons (i.e. Seasons 1-6, 10).
