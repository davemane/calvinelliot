There's an old saying: "Give an inch and they'll take a mile." Or in some cases, miles and miles of miles and miles.

The saying is probably more prominent inside of conservative circles who loath the idea of the government taking the metaphorical "inch" which quickly becomes a mile. I want to be careful by saying I generally agree with the sentiment behind the saying, but I'd be remiss if I didn't mention how overblown the phrase has become. Too many people have often looked at subtle change as the "inch" which leads to something much bigger. That said, the saying fits perfectly when considering the measures taken to combat COVID-19.

What started as proposed, two-week "flatten the curve" measures, quickly evolved into a over a year of quarantining, masking, and a rush to produce a vaccine.
