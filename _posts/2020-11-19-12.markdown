The housing market is hot. It's been warming up for some time now, but the Federal Reserve dropping the federal funds rate to zero percent in response to the COVID-19 pandemic has fueled the mortgage industry to experience demand not seen since the 2000s. Consumers getting historically cheap mortgages has led to refinances and new mortgage originations in a way that seems unsustainable. But is it?
